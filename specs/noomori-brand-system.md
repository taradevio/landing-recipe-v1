# Noomori Brand System

**Status:** Implementation-ready brand source of truth  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** Landing page, mobile app, onboarding, product screenshots, motion, marketing assets  
**Primary references used for logic only:** Gamma, Lassie, Dia, attached health UI reference, attached Leafora UI reference  
**Brand authority:** Noomori product truth and current Noomori palette always override external references.

---

## 1. Brand North Star

> **A warm shared kitchen, translated into a digital product.**

Noomori is not visually or verbally positioned as:

- an AI-first recipe app;
- a generic recipe database;
- a productivity dashboard;
- a Pinterest-style recipe feed;
- a generic SaaS product;
- a luxury fine-dining editorial brand.

Noomori should feel like a dependable place where recipes live, get improved, and are shared with the people someone cooks with.

### Core emotional object

> **The recipes a home comes back to.**

### Working brand promise

> **Keep the recipes your home comes back to.**

### Supporting product statement

> Save recipes from anywhere, make them your own, and keep one reliable collection with the people you cook with.

---

## 2. Brand Personality

The intended personality balance is:

| Trait | Weight | Expression |
|---|---:|---|
| Warm | 35% | Off-white surfaces, food imagery, human copy, generous spacing |
| Reliable / Clear | 30% | Strong hierarchy, predictable navigation, legible states |
| Playful | 20% | Gentle motion, small doodle-like marks, friendly shapes |
| Modern | 15% | Contemporary layout, polished spacing, restrained UI |

### Governing rule

**Warmth attracts. Clarity earns trust. Playfulness adds personality. Modernity keeps the system contemporary.**

Playfulness must never overpower usability.

---

## 3. Primary Brand Anchor — The Warm Kitchen Table

The core metaphor is:

> **The Warm Kitchen Table**

Imagine a modern kitchen table in soft morning or late-afternoon light. It contains recipes, notes, food photography, and familiar shared objects. It feels lived-in without feeling messy.

The visual idea is:

> **Recipes gathered around one shared place.**

This metaphor may influence:

- landing page composition;
- hero animation;
- onboarding;
- household screens;
- empty states;
- screenshots;
- campaign imagery;
- motion;
- copywriting.

### Do not translate the metaphor literally into

- wood textures everywhere;
- rustic farmhouse styling;
- scrapbook UI;
- torn-paper effects;
- faux handwritten recipe cards as a default;
- nostalgia-heavy “grandma cookbook” design.

Noomori remains a modern digital product.

---

## 4. Visual Signature — Kitchen Light

**Kitchen Light** is Noomori’s primary expressive visual device.

### Definition

A soft, organic gradient that behaves like natural light falling across a kitchen surface, recipe card, or food scene.

### Preferred gradient directions

- deep teal → teal → mint → warm cream;
- warm cream → amber → soft terracotta;
- teal/mint with a restrained warm edge.

### Avoid

- purple-blue-pink AI gradients;
- neon aurora effects;
- high-saturation rainbow gradients;
- multiple unrelated gradients in one viewport;
- sharp or synthetic digital lighting.

### Usage

Kitchen Light may appear in:

- landing hero;
- selected feature showcases;
- CTA sections;
- onboarding illustrations;
- selected empty states;
- branded marketing imagery.

It should **not** appear behind every card or section.

## 7. Product Story

The primary product narrative is:

> **Capture → Make It Yours → Cook Together**

### 01 — Capture

Core idea:

> **Found a recipe? Keep it.**

Noomori helps users bring recipes in from:

- websites;
- pasted text;
- manual creation.

Import is a feature, not the identity.

### 02 — Make It Yours

Core idea:

> **Make every recipe yours.**

Imported recipes become editable cooking objects.

Users should be able to:

- clean up ingredients;
- adjust quantities or units;
- edit instructions;
- reorder steps;
- add notes;
- maintain the version that actually works.

### 03 — Cook Together

Core idea:

> **Recipes are better when everyone can find them.**

Household is a core value, not a premium collaboration add-on.

It should feel like:

- one shared kitchen;
- one reliable recipe collection;
- clear recipe source/context;
- human sharing.

It should not feel like:

- enterprise workspaces;
- permission dashboards;
- team administration.

## 8. Graphic Devices

Noomori should use a small number of recognizable devices.

### Device 1 — Kitchen Light

Primary atmospheric device.

### Device 2 — Recipe Marks

Small hand-drawn accents such as:

- curved underline;
- tiny circle;
- small spark;
- check mark;
- short curved arrow;
- ingredient tick.

### Rules

- use sparingly;
- keep them away from critical controls;
- never combine doodles + stickers + grain + 3D blobs + watercolor + random illustration styles;
- decoration should support hierarchy, never become the hierarchy.

---

## 9. Photography Direction

Food should behave as primary content.

### Preferred

- warm;
- natural;
- close;
- appetizing;
- slightly imperfect;
- household-real;
- textural;
- believable.

### Avoid

- generic stock cooking lifestyle imagery;
- ultra-luxury black-background food photography;
- over-processed imagery;
- imagery whose lighting fights the Noomori palette.

### Marketing ratio guideline

- **70% actual product UI**
- **20% food / household context**
- **10% graphic devices**

### User-generated recipe imagery

Use normal rectangular crops by default.

Isolated food/object cutouts may be used selectively for:

- onboarding;
- empty states;
- category art;
- campaign imagery.

## 10. Motion Language

Core motion principle:

> **Things gently find their place.**

Examples:

- source recipe moves into Noomori;
- cards shift to make space for a newly saved recipe;
- ingredients snap into layout;
- household recipe appears in the shared collection;
- Kitchen Light shifts slowly in the background.

### Timing guidance

| Motion type | Duration |
|---|---:|
| Micro interaction | 150–220 ms |
| Layout transition | 300–450 ms |
| Story / feature demo | 600–1000 ms |
| Ambient Kitchen Light | slow, continuous |

### Avoid

- scroll-jacking;
- physics-heavy bouncing;
- animation on every card;
- excessive parallax;
- motion that delays content access;
- word-by-word hero text animation.

Playful does not mean frantic.

## 11. Copywriting Voice

Noomori should sound:

- human;
- concise;
- warm;
- useful;
- calm;
- lightly playful.

### Good territory

- “Found a recipe? Keep it.”
- “No more digging through old bookmarks.”
- “Recipes are better when everyone can find them.”
- “Keep the recipes your home comes back to.”
- “Bring your recipes home.”

### Avoid

- “Streamline your culinary workflow.”
- “Effortlessly manage your recipes.”
- “Supercharge your cooking experience.”
- “Unlock the power of AI.”
- “Revolutionize your kitchen.”

### Rule

Noomori should sound like someone who understands cooking and household friction—not like SaaS marketing copy.

## 13. Mobile Brand Expression

Noomori mobile is:

> **A warm editorial recipe experience built on top of a clear utility system.**

It should not become:

- a Pinterest-style visual feed;
- a dense productivity dashboard;
- a card-heavy settings app;
- an ecommerce UI.

### Mobile emphasis

- warm neutral canvas;
- medium-low information density;
- image-first recipe presentation;
- quiet navigation;
- medium-soft rounded surfaces;
- clear metadata;
- utility before decoration.

---

## 14. Brand Do / Don’t

### Do

- use real product UI as proof;
- keep household human;
- let food imagery carry emotional weight;
- use gradients selectively;
- maintain one visual anchor;
- use hierarchy before decoration;
- keep mobile quieter than marketing;
- preserve credibility with imperfect data.

### Don’t

- copy Gamma, Lassie, Dia, Leafora, or the health reference literally;
- lead with AI;
- use every palette color in one screen;
- turn every section into a card;
- use fake product UI in marketing;
- overload recipe cards;
- make household feel like a workspace;
- let a new reference redefine the brand.

---

## 17. Brand Acceptance Checklist

A design belongs to Noomori when:

- it can be described without mentioning reference brands;
- it feels warm without becoming childish;
- it feels reliable enough for recipes users care about;
- product UI is clearer than decoration;
- household feels human;
- food imagery supports the story;
- the palette is restrained;
- motion has a purpose;
- references are visible only as logic, not imitation;
- imperfect data does not visually break the experience.
